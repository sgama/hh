Managed paid social campaigns end-to-end in Meta Ads Manager for a fashion e-commerce brand — from audience structure to ad creative and copy.

**Objective:** bring qualified traffic to the online store through Meta's ad network.

**Approach:** built campaign structures around audience targeting, produced ad creative and copy in-house, and planned the campaign calendar.

**Channels & tools:** Meta Ads Manager (paid social), Google Analytics 4 (GA4) for performance tracking and customer engagement analysis.

**Result:** a repeatable process — targeting → creative → launch → measurement — reused across campaigns.
